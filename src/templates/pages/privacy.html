Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 23, 2026

About This Service

KernelWorx is a volunteer-run application designed to help Scouting America units manage their popcorn fundraising campaigns. This service is provided free of charge to support youth development programs.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide the service:

  • Account Information: Email address, name, and authentication credentials (if using email/password login)
  • Seller Profiles: Names of Scouts you create profiles for (first name, last name, optional unit/den information)
  • Campaign Data: Product catalogs, orders, customer information (names, amounts), and sales reports
  • Social Login: If you use Google or Facebook to sign in, we receive your email and name from those providers

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to:

  • Provide the popcorn sales management service
  • Allow you to track and manage fundraising campaigns
  • Enable sharing of profiles with other adult leaders (when you grant permission)
  • Generate sales reports and QR codes for payment collection

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely in AWS (Amazon Web Services) using industry-standard encryption. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties except as required to operate the service (e.g., AWS hosting).

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as you maintain an active account. You may delete your account and all associated data at any time (see "Your Data Deletion Rights" below).

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS): Cloud hosting, database, authentication (Cognito), and file storage
  • Google Sign-In (optional): Social login authentication - subject to Google's privacy policy

Your Data Deletion Rights

You have the right to delete your account and all associated data at any time. This will permanently remove:

  • Your account and login credentials
  • All seller profiles you created
  • All campaigns, orders, and customer data
  • All reports and uploaded files (QR codes, etc.)
  • All shares and invitations

How to Delete Your Data

Option 1: Self-Service (Recommended)

  • 1. Log in to your account
  • 2. Go to Settings (click your profile icon in the top right)
  • 3. Click 'Account Settings'
  • 4. Scroll to the bottom and click 'Delete My Account'
  • 5. Confirm the deletion (this action cannot be undone)

Option 2: Contact Us

If you're unable to access your account or prefer assistance, send an email to:

privacy@kernelworx.app

⚠️ Important: You must send the email from the email address registered to your account. We verify account deletion requests by email address only.

Note: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Please export any reports you need before deleting your account.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, please contact us at:

privacy@kernelworx.app

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Volunteer Service: This application is provided as a free volunteer service to support Scouting America fundraising activities. While we strive to maintain the highest standards of data security and privacy, this service is provided "as is" without warranties.
